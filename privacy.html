Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026. OpenClaw Drive Archive is a personal tool operated by the Google account owner on a local Mac mini. It is not a service for other users.

Data accessed and purpose

After the owner grants Google OAuth access, the tool may read Google Drive file names, metadata and contents, and create or update files. It uses that access to archive owner-selected articles, verify that uploads match local copies, and store analysis notes in the owner's configured Drive folders. The OAuth permission technically permits broader Drive access; the tool's intended operations are limited to these archive and analysis workflows.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored in the local rclone configuration on the owner's Mac mini, not on this website. Article files and analysis notes are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Operational logs or temporary copies may remain on the local computer. The tool does not sell Google user data or create public Drive sharing links by default. This website does not receive or store Google Drive data.

Article analysis

When the owner requests article analysis, the selected article's text and metadata may be sent to the AI model provider configured in OpenClaw to produce an analysis. Google OAuth credentials are not included in the model prompt. The model provider's own privacy terms govern its processing.

Retention and control

Archived files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner removes them. The owner can revoke this app's Google access in their Google Account settings and remove local OAuth credentials from rclone. Revoking access does not automatically delete files already saved in Drive.

Contact

For questions, use the support email address shown on this app's Google OAuth consent screen.
